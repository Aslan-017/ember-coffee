How it started

A calm room
in a fast city.

EMBER opened because we wanted somewhere to sit that did not ask us to leave. Astana moves quickly, and a good cup of coffee is one of the few honest reasons to slow down for twenty minutes.

So we kept the room simple: wooden tables, cream walls, and windows that catch the morning light. The coffee does the talking, and the details we fuss over are small ones - water temperature, the weight of a cup, how long a croissant sits before it is served.

A morning at EMBER, in order:

  1. Beans are weighed and ground for the first shot at 07:40
  2. The bar tastes the espresso and adjusts the grind
  3. Bread comes out of the oven just before we open
  4. The doors open at 08:00, and the room fills slowly
