Our philosophy

Coffee should feel
like a ritual.

We buy small lots from roasters we actually know, and we taste every batch before it reaches the bar. Nothing here is rushed, because a cup made in a hurry tastes like one.

What we care about, in order:

  • Beans chosen for one season, not for the whole year
  • Preparation that follows the coffee instead of the clock
  • A room quiet enough to hear the person across the table
  • Staying a little longer than you planned
